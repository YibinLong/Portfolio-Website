When you're writing on the internet, sometimes it's pretty easy to forget that Your audience is not just The person that you're replying to, but Potentially thousands of people That are coming across your comment. What that means is that Not everyone is gonna have the exact same knowledge and background context necessary to know what you're talking about when you use acronyms. 

Therefore, something I propose that you should probably do when you're writing a comment is to actually expand upon the com the acronym um for the first time that you're using it. N not everyone knows the jargon of the field you are involved in, and sometimes This adds unnecessary friction to curious people who want to know more about your field to know what the heck is going on.

If even Elon is getting frustrated and confused about his employee's (unnecessary) Use of acronyms, You should consider "dumbing" your writing down for the Wider audience! it'll increase the comprehensibility of your writing and increase people's understanding!

That being said, uh I've had a pretty simple strategy whenever I see an acronym that I don't understand, usually on Twitter/X. I just copy and paste the tweet and then just simply ask it what's the what does [acronym] stand for? I know it's really simple, but before my eyes would simply glaze over and I wouldn't understand anything about the topic. But now I glean much more information and I actually learn a lot of useful things in fields that I wasn't familiar at all with. So I highly recommend it!
